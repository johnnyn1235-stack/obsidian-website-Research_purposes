### Mechanism of Action
---
#### Direct
Non-competitive functional inhibitor DKK1 (Dickkopf-1) to preventing it from binding to LRP5/6 co-receptors and prevents the formation of the DKK1-LRP5-Kremen inhibitory complex activating [[Wnt β-catenin]] signaling.
