### About
---
Thiazide diuretics are prescription water pills like [[Hydrochlorothiazide]], chlorthalidone, and indapamide used primarily to treat high blood pressure and fluid swelling.

### Mechanism of Action
---
Blocking salt and water reabsorption in the early part of the kidney's distal convoluted tubule, lower total fluid volume in the body to reduce heart strain, and open up blood vessels over time to keep blood pressure down.

### Common Use Cases
---
Hypertension 
Mild fluid build-up or swelling from heart failure
Lowering calcium levels in urine to prevent certain kidney stones.
